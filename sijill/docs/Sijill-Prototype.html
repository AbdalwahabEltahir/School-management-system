What Sijill keeps, and what it does not

  • Keeps a short profile of each file: its type, sensitivity, location, sharing and owner.
  • Does not keep copies of file contents.
  • Hides names and ID numbers before any text is sent to the AI.
  • Cannot change, move or delete files: its Google access is read-only.
  • Sees only the EAA Google Workspace: not personal email, phones, laptops or WhatsApp.
